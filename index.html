Privacy Policy – Video CME

Charak Pharma Pvt Ltd · Effective date: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Charak Pharma Pvt Ltd (“Charak”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal data through the Video CME mobile application (“the App”, package com.charakpharma.livomyn) and its related web services.

Who uses the App. The App is a business tool for Charak's authorised business partners (Direct Selling Associates, “DSAs”) and the area and regional managers who coordinate with them (together, “authorised users”). It is used to conduct a short continuing medical education (CME) session with healthcare professionals (“doctors”): the doctor watches a scientific video, answers a short survey, and receives a certificate of participation. There is no public sign-up; login accounts are created and managed by Charak for authorised users only.

1. Information we collect

a) About authorised users of the App (DSAs and managers)

  • Login username (usually the user's mobile number) and password. The password is checked on our server and is never shown or logged. If the user chooses “Remember me”, it is kept on that phone in the device's encrypted secure storage.
  • Name, designation, headquarters (HQ), zone and reporting managers' names, HQs and mobile numbers, as held in Charak's business partner records.
  • Date and time of the latest login, and a record of the sessions the user conducts.

b) About doctors who take part in a CME session

  • Name, mobile number, email address, qualification/degree, classification and HQ.
  • A photograph of the doctor, taken with the phone's camera (or chosen from the gallery) with the doctor's agreement, which is printed on the certificate.
  • Survey answers (for example, the clinical indications the doctor selects).
  • How much of the session video was played, and when each step of the session took place.
  • The certificate issued (certificate number, image and PDF).

c) Technical information

  • The type of platform used (Android, iOS or web), recorded with session activity.
  • Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability.

The App does not collect location data, contacts, call logs or SMS, does not record audio, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics/tracking tools.

3. How we use the information

  • To let authorised users log in and to show them the doctors and data relevant to their HQ and team.
  • To conduct the CME session, issue the doctor's certificate and let the doctor receive it.
  • To keep records of sessions held, and to report progress (for example, completed and pending sessions) to the DSA's managers and to Charak administrators.
  • To understand doctors' feedback on the scientific session in aggregate.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How information is shared

  • Within Charak: session records are visible to the DSA who conducted the session, to their managers (ASM/RSM) and to authorised administrators.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract: Supabase (database, login and server functions) and Cloudinary (storage of certificate files, which include the doctor's photograph). These providers may store data on servers located outside India.
  • With the doctor, when the DSA shares the certificate – for example through WhatsApp or another app chosen on the phone. That sharing is done by the DSA through the chosen app, and the chosen app's own privacy policy applies to it. A certificate link sent this way opens the certificate file for anyone who has the link.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Charak, our business partners or others.

5. Storage on the phone

To work smoothly in the field, the App keeps on the phone: the session video (so it is not downloaded again), the logged-in user's profile for the day, and – only if chosen – the saved login details in encrypted storage. Logging out clears the day's login. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all of these.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to records is controlled on the server so that each user can see only the data they are permitted to see. Passwords and server keys are never stored in the App's code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect personal data.

7. How long we keep data

We keep session records, survey answers and certificates (with the doctor's photograph printed on them) for as long as needed for the CME programme, for business record-keeping and to meet legal requirements. When no longer needed, the data is deleted or anonymised. A user's login access is removed when the business association ends or the user no longer needs the App.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to: access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, delete your data, or withdraw a consent you gave earlier (this does not affect processing done before withdrawal). Doctors who would like their photograph, certificate or other details removed can ask the DSA who conducted the session or their Charak area manager, and authorised users who would like their account or data deleted can ask their Charak manager; the request will be passed to Charak for action. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

9. Children

The App is intended only for adult healthcare professionals and Charak's authorised users. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
